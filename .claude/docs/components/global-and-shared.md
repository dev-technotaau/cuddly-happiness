# Global chrome & shared components

The persistent shell around every page (Header with announcement marquee, mega menu and mobile menu; Footer; the film-grain overlay) and the small shared building blocks reused across pages (ProductCard, Price, WishButton, RecentlyViewed / RecentlyViewedStrip, SectionHeader, useReveal, SmoothScroll/getLenis, lineDetails). CartDrawer is only summarised here; its full behaviour is in the cart doc.

Scope: `src/components/Header.tsx`, `src/components/menu/SiteMenu.tsx`, `src/components/menu/Burger.tsx`, `src/components/menu/search.ts`, `src/components/menu/useHouseSearch.ts`, `src/components/menu/HeaderSearch.tsx`, `src/components/menu/MobileSearch.tsx`, `src/components/Footer.tsx`, `src/components/ProductCard.tsx`, `src/components/Price.tsx`, `src/components/WishButton.tsx`, `src/components/RecentlyViewed.tsx`, `src/components/home/SectionHeader.tsx`, `src/components/SmoothScroll.tsx`, `src/components/useReveal.ts`, `src/components/lineDetails.ts`, `src/App.tsx` (film grain), `src/components/CartDrawer.tsx` (summary)

### 2.1 Desktop menu (`src/components/menu/SiteMenu.tsx`, added 2026-10-20)

An addition to the bar, not a replacement. The bar keeps its links and mega menu; the menu repeats them and has room for the pages the bar can't hold. `Header` renders `<SiteMenu open focus onClose trigger onCover />` through `lazy()` and `<Suspense fallback={null}>`, mounted closed ahead of the first click: when the pointer or focus reaches the menu button (`prepareMenu`), and 2.5 s after load on desktop. Mounting on click made the first opening lag about 400 ms, because a lazy component suspends on its first render even with its chunk cached, and React holds that first reveal back. The menu portals to `<body>`.

**Opening.**
- The button: `openMenu(e.detail === 0 ? 'keyboard' : 'pointer')`, which also closes the mega menu.
- Shortcuts: until 2026-10-22, `/` and ⌘K / Ctrl+K opened it into its search (`focus = 'search'`); they now focus the bar's search (§2.2), and the `'search'` focus has no caller. Inside the open menu, `/` and ⌘K still focus its own search. They are ignored while the menu is open, while typing in an input, textarea, select or contenteditable, below 1024 px, and while any other visible, non-inert `[role="dialog"]` is open.
- Initial focus, 50 ms after opening: the search (`'search'`), the first rail item (`'keyboard'`), or the dialog itself, `tabIndex={-1}` (`'pointer'`).
- Every opening starts fresh: no panel or query, the salon status recomputed, the menu button re-measured.

**Layout.** `fixed inset-0 z-[70]`, `role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-label="Menu" id="site-menu"`, `inert` while closed.
- Backdrop: `bg-[var(--bg-vault)]/45` with `backdrop-blur-[3px]`, both fading over 500 ms. A click closes.
- **Rail**, `w-[clamp(340px,30vw,420px)]`, full height, travertine, shadow `24px 0 60px -30px`. From the top:
  - A vault strip as tall as the announcement bar (measured from the menu button) with the salon's live status: a dot (champagne when open) and `salonStatus().label`, for example `Open now · until 18h` ([data-model.md](../data-model.md) §9a).
  - An 80 px row level with the header bar, its hairline continuing the header's: room for the close cross, then the search field (`Search` icon, placeholder `Search the maison`, `aria-label="Search pieces, chapters and pages"`, a `/` keycap, or `Clear` while there is a query).
  - The nav (`aria-label="Main menu"`, `data-menu-list="rail"`): the `RAIL` entries **Shop**, **Bridal**, **Rentals**, **Design your ring** (2026-10-22), **The Atelier** in `font-serif-editorial text-[clamp(28px,2.4vw,38px)] font-light`. Each shows its French name (`La boutique`, `Mariage`, `Location`, `Sur mesure`, `L’Atelier`) in italic when hovered, focused, current or unfolded; hovering one dims the others to taupe. Shop is a button (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls="menu-extension"`, a gold `ArrowRight`); the others are links with `aria-current="page"` when current.
  - A shelf of three 4:5 thumbnails with names: **Recently viewed** (from `recent`, minus the piece on screen, with a `Clear` button that calls `clearRecent` and keeps focus in the dialog), else **New arrivals** (the first three products badged `New`).
  - A hairline-topped footer: three tiles in a hairline grid, **Wishlist** (count badge), the account (**Sign in**, or the first name with a gold dot) and **Bag** (count badge; closes the menu, then `setCartOpen(true)`). Then the espresso button **Book an appointment** (`href.atelier`), and a wrapping row with `LanguageToggle` (`EN` · `FR`), the currency switch (`role="group" aria-label="Currency"`, `aria-pressed`) and `SALON.phone` as a `tel:` link.
  - Every label is bilingual (`COPY`); the rail's accents in French are `les chapitres`, `pour la vie`, `le temps d’un soir`, `sur mesure`, `rue Sherbrooke`. Search indexes both languages (French and English names, categories, metals, badges, bilingual `KEYWORDS`), so `ring` finds `Bagues` on the French site.

  - Up to 1600 px the cross sits above the rail at the menu button's measured position (`useLayoutEffect`, before the first paint), so the two lines fold into a cross in place. On close it folds back; 500 ms later the cross and the button swap in a single commit (`standIn` and `onCover(false)` together).
  - Past 1600 px the header's container is centred and the button lies beyond the rail's corner. The cross is then rendered inside the rail at its corner (`left: 39`) and travels with it, and the button returns as soon as the menu starts closing.

  - The list, `w-[clamp(300px,26vw,380px)]`, under its own vault strip and an 80 px heading row: `Shop` *La boutique* with `9 chapters`, or `{n} results for “{q}”` (`aria-live="polite"`). - A gold hairline glides beside the rail (to the hovered or focused item, else the unfolded parent or the current page) and beside the list (to the active row).

**Behaviour.**
- **Hover** (mouse only): a parent unfolds its panel after 80 ms; a plain link folds it after 200 ms. Menu aim: while the pointer travels right (samples from the last 150 ms), the fold waits and re-checks every 120 ms, so a diagonal path across Bridal into the panel keeps it open. Entering the extension cancels any pending switch.
- **Clicks**: a mouse click on Shop only unfolds; keys and taps toggle.
- **Active row**: the hovered or focused one, else the current page's chapter (or Gift Cards), else the first; in search, the first result.
- **Search** (since 2026-10-22 through the shared engine, §2.3, which adds plurals, synonyms, typos, prices and renting; the ranking below is `rank` in `search.ts`): case- and accent-insensitive (`fold`), and every word must match. A word scores 3 at the start of a field, 2 at the start of a word, 1 inside a word (only for words of six letters or more, so "rings" doesn't find earrings), times the field weight:
  - pieces: name 10, the other language's name 7, collection 6, kind in both languages ("Engagement ring") 5, chapter names and accent 4, tagline 3, metal labels and badges in both languages 2, description, spec values and the other tagline 1;
  - chapters: name 10, the other language's name 7, accent 6, the other accent 3, description 2; the two signature pages rank with them (name 10, the other name 7, line 2);
  - pages: title 10, `KEYWORDS` 4.

  The fields live in `search.ts` (`pieceFields`, `chapterFields`, `signatureFields`, `KEYWORDS`), shared with the phone search (`MobileSearch`) so both rank alike; the highlight is `Marked.tsx`.

  A title that starts with the whole query (as typed, or without its filler) gets +25. Results are grouped Chapters (3), Pieces (6) and Elsewhere (2 of the ring designer, The Atelier, Gift Cards, Wishlist, the account, the bag, Home; the two actions have their own buttons below the rail), with matches underlined in gold (`marks`). With no result: `Nothing matches “{q}”.`, `Try a stone, a metal or a Montréal place:` and chips **Diamond**, **Pearl**, **Emerald**, **Mont-Royal**, **Platinum**.
- **Keyboard**: Escape clears the query, then folds the panel (focus to its parent), then closes. ↑/↓/Home/End move within a list (from the dialog itself, ↑/↓ enter the rail). → on a parent unfolds it and focuses its current or first row; ← in the extension goes back to the parent (or the search). In the search, ↓ enters the results and Enter opens the first one. ⌘K / Ctrl+K and `/` focus the search, and any other printable key types into it. Tab is trapped, skipping inert parts.
- **Closing**: the cross, the backdrop, Escape, every link (`onClick={onClose}`), a route change (`Header`'s `[route]` effect), or the viewport dropping below 1024 px. Focus returns to the menu button unless something else took it (the Bag tile hands it to the drawer).
- **While open**: `getLenis()?.stop()` and `body` overflow `hidden`, both restored on close; the rail's middle and the list carry `data-lenis-prevent`.
- **Reduced motion**: the rail fades instead of sliding, the extension appears without the wipe, the glyph folds at once, items fade without rising, photographs cross-fade without the settle.
- **Adding pages**: a new top-level page joins `RAIL`; one with sub-pages gets a `panel` key and an entry in `panels` (a title, French title, aside and `Dest` links), and unfolds like Shop. Add a `pages` entry here, a `doorList` entry in `useHouseSearch.ts` and its `KEYWORDS` line in `search.ts`, so every search (the bar, this menu, phones) finds it.

### 2.2 Search in the bar (`src/components/menu/HeaderSearch.tsx`, added 2026-10-22)

The owner asked for Atelier to leave the bar (it stays in the desktop menu's rail, the mobile menu and the footer) and for "a top notch … luxury search bar there with … full flagged search mega dropdown". `Header` renders `<HeaderSearch open={searchOpen} onOpenChange={openSearch} field={searchField} />` at the end of the nav, after Rentals.

**The field, closed.** A `flex-1` cell inside a `role="search"` landmark, at least 44 px (`min-w-11`, a touch target on tablets) and at most `max-w-[26rem]`: an `h-10` field on a hairline underline (taupe on hover) with a 15 px `Search` icon (vermeil on hover). It is a container (`@container`), so its words follow its room: the icon alone, then `Search` / `Rechercher` in the serif's italic (15 px, taupe; from 72 px, 100 px in French), then `Search the maison` / `Rechercher dans la maison` (from 200 px, 256 px in French), and the `/` key from 160 px. French keeps the bar's compact sizes until 1366 px, with 12 px gaps (`roomy` in `Header`), so the field keeps its word at 1280 and its 44 px at 1024. Closed but focused (after Escape), it shows its caret and an espresso underline.

**Opening.** Focus opens it: a click, Tab, or `/` and ⌘K / Ctrl+K (`Header`'s window shortcut: desktop only, not while typing, not with another dialog open). Opening closes the mega menu; opening the desktop menu closes the search. While open:
- the links fold away (a `grid` track from `1fr` to `0fr` over 500 ms, clipped, `inert`), so the field takes the bar; typed text is the serif at 18 px, and a gold line draws across the underline (700 ms);
- an empty field shows one idea at a time, every 3.2 s: `Try` *tennis necklaces* · *an oval solitaire* · *a gift under $1,000* · *Mont-Royal* · *pearls* · *a platinum band* (French `Cherchez` *des colliers tennis*, *un cadeau sous 1 000 …); the idea truncates, never the `Try`, and the Esc key gives way to it below 288 px; still under reduced motion;
- the page dims (`bg-[var(--bg-vault)]/30`, `backdrop-blur-[2px]`) and holds still with `getLenis()?.stop()` alone, which swallows the wheel. The body is not locked, so the scrollbar stays and the bar never shifts sideways (an exception to the overlay checklist: this is a dropdown under the sticky header, not a dialog);
- the dropdown (`#header-search`, `absolute inset-x-0 top-full` under the bar, travertine, hairline, a soft shadow) is unveiled by `clip-path` over 700 ms; each state fades up as it changes.

**Empty.** Three columns (3 · 6 · 3 of 12): **Recent searches** (up to four, with a `Clear` option; the store's `searches`, `tns-searches`) above **Begin with** (six, or four under recent searches: Engagement rings, Tennis necklaces, Wedding bands, Gifts under {`formatPrice(1000)`}, To rent, Pearls); **Our signatures**, the two signature pages as 4:3 photographs with `Signature I`/`II`, the name (26 px serif) and `{n} pieces · from {Price}`; **Recently viewed** (three, not the piece on screen), else **New arrivals**.

**Typing.** `useHouseSearch().find(query, currency)` (§2.3) answers each keystroke:
- what was read, above the results: `Showing results for *tennis*` (a typo corrected), a price chip (`Under $1,000`, `$500 – $2,000`, `$5,000 and over`) and `To rent`, each with × to take it out of the query;
- on the left (3/12): **Suggestions** (up to four completions with their piece counts), **Chapters** (up to three: signature pages and chapters), **Elsewhere** (up to two: pages, and the two actions `Book a visit` → `openVisit()` and `Ask a jeweller` → `openAsk()`); long names wrap to two lines;
- on the right (9/12): `{n} pieces` and the cards, four from 1280 px (three below), six when the left column is empty (four below): a 4:5 photograph (the second, on the body, on the active card, with a gold line and the zoom), `collection · chapter`, the name with the match underlined (`Marked`), the price and, for a piece that rents, `Or rent from {weekend rate} a weekend`; a search for renting shows the rate first. `See all {n}` (`href.shop({ q })`) appears when some are left out at that width;
- nothing found: `Nothing matches “{q}”.`, the five chips (Diamond, Pearl, Emerald, Mont-Royal, Platinum) and the signatures;
- the footer: the keys (↑ ↓ Move · ↵ Open · Esc Close, `aria-hidden`), `Can’t find it? The atelier can often source or make it.` (from 1280 px) and **Ask a jeweller**.

**Keys and focus.**
- The input is a `role="combobox"` (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls="header-search-list"`, `aria-autocomplete="list"`, `aria-activedescendant`). The dropdown's body is that `role="listbox"` (`#header-search-list`) of `role="group"`s labelled by their eyebrows; its links and buttons are `role="option"` with `tabIndex={-1}`. ↑/↓ move through the options on screen (cards hidden at the width are skipped) and pass back through the field after the last; the pointer moves the same highlight (silk rows, the gold line on cards). A `role="status"` line counts results.
- Enter opens the active option, else the query's landing (below). Escape clears the query, then closes (the focus stays), then leaves the field; on a control inside the dropdown (a chip, **Ask a jeweller**) it first returns to the field. The recent searches' `Clear` is named `Clear recent searches`. ⌘K / Ctrl+K in the field selects its text.
- Presses inside the field and the dropdown keep the focus in the field (`preventDefault` on mousedown), so typing carries on after a click. Focus leaving both closes it: Tab past `Ask a jeweller`, a press on the dimmed page, anything else.
- A result followed is remembered (`rememberSearch`) and the search goes stale (so does a new page, or a page rewriting its address, `tns:address`, as when the search chip is taken out): the dropdown keeps its content while it folds away, and the next focus starts empty, except on the search's own page (`#/shop?q=`), where the field picks the search up to refine it. Closed, the bar never shows an old query.

**Enter's landing** (`Found.landing`), followed through a hidden link so the page transition plays: an exact name first, in either language (`keyOf`): a chapter or its alias ("rent", "vault" → Rentals; "wedding" → Bridal; "gift" → Gifts; "custom", "sur mesure" → Sur mesure), a signature, a kind of piece to its chapter filtered ("studs" → `#/c/earrings?type=studs`, "wedding bands" → `#/c/bridal?type=band`, "tennis necklaces" → the signature page), a page or an action ("appointment" opens the visit drawer, "gift card" the gift-card page), a piece by name. Else the one piece; else every match on `#/shop?q=`; else the first chapter or page. With nothing found, Enter does nothing.

### 2.3 The search engine (`search.ts`, `useHouseSearch.ts`, 2026-10-22)

One engine serves the bar's search, the desktop menu's search (which shows its chapters and pages as its own destinations, with their photographs), phones (`MobileSearch`) and the shop page's `?q=`.
- **Index** (`useHouseSearch`, rebuilt per language and sign-in): the two signature pages and the nine chapters (`Way`); the pieces, except the gift card (a page); the pages and two actions (`Door`: `a:visit`, `a:ask`, `g:atelier`, `g:giftcards`, `g:wishlist`, `g:account`, `g:cart`, `g:home`), with titles in both languages and their `KEYWORDS`. Fields and weights as in §2.1.
- **Reading a query** (`read`): words are folded (case, accents, `’` → `'`) and split at spaces, apostrophes and punctuation. Filler goes, in both languages ("a ring for her" → ring; "or" stays: in French it is gold). A price is read out: `under`, `below`, `less than`, `up to`, `max`, `<`; `over`, `above`, `more than`, `at least`, `>`; `between … and …`; a bare `$500–$2k`; `$1,000`, `1 000 $`, `2.5k`; French `moins de`, `sous`, `jusqu’à`, `plus de`, `au moins`, `à partir de`, `dès`, `entre … et …`, `de … à …`. A bare number stays a word ("Automatic 38", "18k gold"). Renting is read out (`rent`, `rentals`, `hire`, `borrow`, `louer`, `emprunter`; `location` only in French, where it means renting). A typo is corrected towards the catalog's own words (one edit for four to six letters, two from seven, a swap counting as one; names and kinds win over descriptions, a whole word over a longer one) and shown as "Showing results for …".
- **Matching** (`rank`, `marks`): every word must match; a word also matches its singular (rings → ring, watches → watch, bijoux → bijou) and a few words the catalog doesn't use (hoops, créoles → huggies; bangle → cuff; choker → collar; proposal → engagement; anniversary → eternity; jewelry → jewellery; present → gift; charm, locket → pendant; pink → rose; timepiece → watch), at 0.8. "silver" is deliberately not mapped: the house works in gold and platinum, and the empty state offers Platinum.
- **Prices** (`rentLed`): a price means the price to own unless the query asks to rent, so a $92,000 collar isn't "under $1,000" by its weekend rate. A price or renting alone lists the dearest first.
- **Completions** (`complete`): kinds of piece, collections, metals and stones in the language on screen, where each word of the query starts a word of the phrase; those that begin with the whole query come first, and a phrase already listed as a chapter is left out.
- **Shopify**: predictive search (`/search/suggest`) returns the same four resources (queries, products, collections, pages); `See all` is the `/search?q=` template, shown here on the shop page. Synonyms belong in Search & Discovery.

**The shop page with `?q=`** (`src/pages/Shop.tsx`; [../pages/shop.md](../pages/shop.md)): the pieces the search finds, best match first (the sort reads "Best match"); the search is the first chip (`“{q}”`, × takes it out) and lives in the toolbar's field (`ShopSearch`, which searches as the visitor types); `Clear all` clears it too, and the Filter badge counts filters only; a search for renting leads the cards with their rates. The hero names it (`Maison / Search`, `“{q}”` in the gold italic, `{n} pieces found`) and keeps that height while the visitor searches on. The tab title (`Search: {q} — Teresa & Sons`) and the transition card (`Search` · `“{q}”`) name it too, and the tab title follows a page's own option changes (`tns:address`).

## 5. BackToTop (`src/components/BackToTop.tsx`)

A floating back-to-top control, mounted once in `src/App.tsx` after `CartDrawer`. A fixed wrapper (`pointer-events-none fixed bottom-[max(1.25rem,env(safe-area-inset-bottom))] right-[max(1.25rem,env(safe-area-inset-right))] z-[45] lg:bottom-8 lg:right-8`; `z-[45]` keeps it above the StickyBar and below the header, whose stacking context holds the mobile menu) holds one round glass button (`rounded-full border backdrop-blur-md`, `h-12`).

| Behaviour | How it works |
|---|---|
| When it shows | When the scrollable range is more than 1.8 viewports and the scroll passes 1.1 vh. It hides again below 0.6 vh. It never shows while the drawer or a scroll-locking overlay is open (`cartOpen`, `lenis.isStopped`, or `overflow: hidden` on html/body); then it is also `inert`. |
| Compact vs expanded | It is a compact 48 px bezel while you read. It opens into a pill, "Back to top", in these cases: for 1.6 s after an upward scroll (smoothed velocity < −1.2 px/frame); while hovered with a mouse or keyboard-focused (`:focus-visible`); during the trip up; and in return mode. A downward scroll collapses it at once. The width animates with `grid-template-columns` 0fr → 1fr. |
| Progress ring | An SVG ring (`r = 17`) in `stroke-[var(--gold-vermeil)]` (champagne on dark) shows `y / limit`. A requestAnimationFrame loop drives it with frame-rate-independent easing (0.35 with Lenis, 0.16 native scroll) and writes straight to `style.strokeDashoffset`. React state never changes per frame. |
| Obstacle lift | An IntersectionObserver tracks elements marked `data-fab-avoid` (the PDP StickyBar and the footer legal row). When one overlaps the button's column and reaches its zone, the wrapper translates up so it sits `GAP` = 12 px above it. The lift is eased, capped at 60% of the viewport and rounded to whole pixels, and the wrapper's box is read back through the lift exactly as written (`applied`), so it settles. Until 2026-10-23 it was read back through the unrounded lift and kept rewriting itself by 0.01 px at the foot of the page, a tremble that showed on hover on scaled screens. |
| Tone | About 8 times a second while visible, `document.elementsFromPoint` at the button's centre finds the first opaque background. Colours are normalised through a 1×1 canvas, so `oklab()`/`color-mix()` values work. Dark backgrounds get `bg-[var(--bg-vault)]/60` glass with a champagne ring; light ones get `bg-[var(--bg-travertine)]/80`. |
| The trip | A click calls `lenis.scrollTo(0, { duration: 0.85 s + 0.12 s per viewport (max 2.2 s), easing: easeInOutQuart })`. The ring unwinds with the scroll. Clicks are ignored while travelling. Without Lenis, or under reduced motion, it jumps instantly. |
| Return offer | If the trip started more than 1.5 viewports down, on arrival the label becomes "Return · {nearest `main h1/h2` above the old position}" (aria-label "Return to …"; "Return to where you were" when no heading is near). The arrow flips 180° and the ring counts down 8 s. A click glides back. The offer lapses after 8 s, or when the reader scrolls past 0.25 vh. |
| Keyboard & focus | Activating it from the keyboard (`detail === 0`) moves focus to the header wordmark link after arrival. Focus shows a 1 px vermeil outline, offset 4 px. |
| Hover | Mouse only. The ring is pulled magnetically up to 5 px and the arrow lifts 0.5 (2 px). Both are disabled under reduced motion. |
| Route change | Resets the mode and the remembered heading. |


## Notes & gotchas

- `Price` always renders in the spec face (Jost, tabular figures): `.font-spec` is declared after `.font-serif-editorial` in `src/index.css`, so passing `font-serif-editorial` in `className` has no effect. Restyle the currency code with `[&_span]:…` utilities (e.g. `[&_span]:hidden`, `[&_span]:text-white/40`).
- Price rules on listing surfaces differ slightly: `ProductCard` requires `category === 'rentals' && rental` and prints "From"; `RecentlyViewedStrip`, video tiles and the try-on panel test only `p.rental` and print no "From". Cards show the base price while the PDP defaults to `metals[0]` (platinum-first bridal pieces open +650 CAD / +475 USD higher).
- Controls nested inside `ProductCard`'s `<a>` (WishButton, TryOnButton) must stay `type="button"` and stop propagation; this is invalid interactive nesting for assistive tech but intentional for the card UX.
- The mobile menu has no scroll lock or Lenis stop (Escape and focus handling were added 2026-10-21).
- Product names, category names, metals and badges display through `useCatalog()` everywhere in this doc's components (`ProductCard` `alt` and `h3` included); the English `product.name` is kept for heuristics only.
- Tailwind v4 wraps `group-hover:` in `@media (hover: hover)`, so the secondary card image never appears on touch devices.
- `useReveal` replays every reveal in scope when its deps change (e.g. Category re-sorting).
